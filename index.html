Privacy & permissions

Camera access

Camera starts only after you tap Start and grant permission. The prototype processes frames in the active browser session and does not intentionally record or upload video.

Notifications

Posture prompts appear inside the app while monitoring is active. System notifications are not implemented.

Background use

Keep the app in the foreground. iOS may suspend the page and camera when you switch apps or lock the screen.

Consent and limitations

You can stop the session at any time. The signals are experimental and this is not a medical device. No diagnosis or treatment advice is provided.

About Posture Guardian

Posture Guardian is a student proof of concept for building posture awareness during device use.

The face signal is a geometry-based facial-position proxy, not a neck angle. Shoulder points are estimated from visible body landmarks; the shoulder-level signal is not a validated measure of shoulder rounding.

Future work could include usability testing, validation against a reference method, accessible reminder settings and properly implemented notifications.
