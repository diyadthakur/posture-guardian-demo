About

Posture Guardian is a student proof of concept for posture awareness during device use.

Face and shoulder signals are experimental proxies, not validated clinical measurements. This is not a medical device.

Research prototype, not a medical device. Camera is accessed only after you press Start and grant browser permission. This page does not record or upload video. The live values are face-landmark measurements and an experimental head-position proxy—not a direct measurement of cervical-spine angle. A modern browser and internet connection are required to load the AI library/model.
